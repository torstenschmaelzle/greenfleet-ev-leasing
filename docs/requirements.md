# Requirements

## Personas

**Marcus, the fleet manager.** 38, runs a fleet of 45 diesel vans and cars at a Swiss logistics company that has committed to CO₂ neutrality by 2030. Data-driven but no EV expert; his main questions are whether EVs can handle his routes and whether the total cost justifies the switch. He wants to go from his requirements to concrete vehicle bundles and then review, sign and keep the contract digitally.

**Lukas, the catalogue manager.** 29, works in GreenFleet's sales support and keeps the vehicle catalogue accurate: adding new models, updating specifications and removing vehicles that can no longer be leased. He values a clean interface for quick, targeted updates.

## User stories

### EV Catalogue
1. Add a vehicle with all technical specifications and the monthly lease price.
2. Update technical details or pricing of a vehicle.
3. Remove a vehicle that can no longer be leased.
4. Record the battery state of health so that lease pricing reflects the battery condition.
5. Search the catalogue by brand, model or VIN.
6. View vehicles grouped by status (available, reserved, under contract, removed).
7. See the catalogue update automatically when a vehicle's status changes.

### Fleet Consulting
8. Enter fleet requirements: daily route, number of vehicles, lease term, charging setup, and optionally budget, brand and vehicle type.
9. Receive scored bundle recommendations (Balanced, Eco, Economic, Premium, Custom).
10. See the total cost of ownership per bundle: monthly lease, charging, monthly and annual total.
11. Specify the charging setup and electricity rate for realistic charging costs.
12. See the annual CO₂ savings compared to a diesel fleet.
13. Rank vehicles by a predefined profile or custom weights for range, CO₂ and cost.
14. See only vehicles that are currently available.
15. Reserve all vehicles of a bundle at once, released automatically if the process does not complete.

### Sales
16. Enter the lessee's contact name, company and business email.
17. Review the complete leasing agreement before signing.
18. Sign the contract with a handwritten digital signature.
19. Cancel a reservation and release the vehicles.
20. See all own contracts with their signing status.
21. Open the full contract document at any time.
22. Print or save a contract as PDF.
23. As an employee, see all contracts across customers.

### Authentication
24. Employees log in with their credentials to reach catalogue management and sales.
25. Fleet managers log in with their customer details to use fleet consulting and their contracts.
