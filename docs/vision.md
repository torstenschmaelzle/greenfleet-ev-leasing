# Product vision

## Vision statement

- **For** small and medium-sized enterprises that operate a commercial vehicle fleet and face growing regulatory and operational pressure to switch to electric mobility,
- **who** are uncertain whether EVs can handle their daily routes, lack transparency about total cost of ownership and charging, and fear the financial risk of the switch,
- **GreenFleet** is an integrated leasing and fleet optimisation platform
- **that** recommends EVs based on real operational requirements, calculates the total cost of ownership including charging, makes CO₂ savings transparent, and handles the whole contract process digitally,
- **unlike** traditional leasing solutions limited to contract administration, generic EV catalogues and isolated cost calculators,
- **our product** guides fleet managers step by step from the first vehicle suggestion to a signed leasing agreement.

## Core features

| Bounded context | Feature |
|---|---|
| EV Catalogue | **Catalogue management:** employees add, update and remove vehicles with all attributes needed for recommendations and leasing. |
| EV Catalogue | **Availability tracking:** every vehicle is available, reserved or under contract; customers only see available vehicles. |
| EV Catalogue | **Reservation and locking:** reserved vehicles are locked and released automatically if no contract follows. |
| Fleet Consulting | **Requirements intake:** daily routes, fleet size, charging setup, budget and vehicle type. |
| Fleet Consulting | **Recommendation engine:** ranked vehicle bundles based on range fit, total cost of ownership, energy prices and CO₂ impact. |
| Sales | **Contract creation:** a leasing contract is generated automatically from the requirements and the selected bundle. |
| Sales | **Contract management:** fleet managers view, sign and print their contracts; employees see all contracts. |
